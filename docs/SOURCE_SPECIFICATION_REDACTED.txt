PART IV — DATABASE INTEGRITY AND BUSINESS RULES

6. NO PHYSICAL DELETION OF HISTORICAL PERSONNEL

Dismissal and external transfer remove people from active-force queries.

They do not destroy their records.

Use logical lifecycle transitions.

Examples:

- ACTIVE
- EXTERNALLY_TRANSFERRED
- DISMISSED

A person's historic name, attendance, previous sites, and movements must remain retrievable.

---

7. FORCE COUNTING RULES

For date D and site S:

"eligible_force(D,S)"

means the number of distinct persons actively assigned to S on D and not already removed by an external departure or dismissal effective on or before D.

For attendance statuses:

"status_count(D,S,status)"

means the number of eligible people having that official status on D.

For pending preparation:

"unprepared(D,S)"

means eligible people without a recorded official attendance state on D.

Required invariant:

"eligible_force = sum(all configured attendance status counts) + unprepared"

Do not treat unprepared personnel as absent.

Do not count an individual twice because the monthly grid displays a movement notification in two sites.

Dashboard counts must be based on distinct eligible people, not on the number of rendered monthly rows.

Historical daily totals must use the assignments and lifecycle events effective on that historical date, not merely the person's current site.

---

8. MONTHLY TRANSFER REPRESENTATION

Transfers are administrative events.

They are not replacements for attendance records.

Suppose person P transfers:

From Site A to Site B on date D.

Required behavior:

Before D

- Site A displays historical daily attendance.
- Site B displays empty cells for P.
- P counts under Site A.

On D

Site A displays:

"تم نقله للموقع (Site B)"

Site B displays:

"تم نقله من (Site A)"

If P has an official attendance status on D, Site B must also display that status in the same date cell, either through a structured two-line cell or a clearly associated transfer annotation.

Do not overwrite the attendance status with a transfer label.

Count P exactly once under Site B on D.

After D

- Site A must not generate further attendance cells for P.
- Site B continues attendance normally.
- Site A retains previous historical values.
- Site B shows no fabricated attendance before D.

Repeated Transfers

Support multiple transfers in the same month.

Each effective date creates a new assignment interval.

Never overwrite old transfers.

If two operations conflict, validate them before committing.

Use one deterministic rule for same-day transfer conflicts, preventing simultaneous contradictory site memberships.

---

9. MONTHLY DEPARTURE AND DISMISSAL REPRESENTATION

When a person is dismissed:

- Remove the individual from the active force.
- Preserve all previous attendance records.
- Display "مفصول" on the dismissal date in the monthly historical participation view.
- Do not create attendance after dismissal.
- Keep the individual available in the dismissal archive.
- Keep the person included in archived historic months where they served.

When externally transferred:

- Remove the person from the active force.
- Preserve the previous service history.
- Preserve the transfer destination.
- Preserve the effective transfer date.
- Keep the individual in the external-transfer archive.
- Do not generate future attendance after departure.

The active personnel registry and the active month's historical accountability projection may have different inclusion rules.

This is intentional.

Do not incorrectly remove a person's earlier monthly attendance simply because they are no longer active today.

10. DATE AND TIME RULES

Use a single centralized date service.

Store business dates as Gregorian civil dates without a time-of-day component.

Store operation timestamps in UTC and convert them to the local display timezone when rendering.

Use Arabic weekday labels.

Automatically calculate:

- 28-day months
- 29-day February
- 30-day months
- 31-day months

The application must handle the configured Windows local timezone consistently.

No daily attendance must be copied from the previous month into the new month.

When the month changes:

1. Detect the previous month.
2. Verify whether it is archived.
3. Archive it exactly once.
4. Seal the archive.
5. Open the new active month.
6. Leave new daily attendance unprepared.
7. Refresh dashboard and monthly-grid projections.

If the program was closed at the month boundary, perform this operation at the next launch before permitting modifications to the new month.

Do not silently modify sealed historical months.
